1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & MISSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SlickMesh AI is an autonomous, end-to-end spaceborne maritime intelligence and
oil spill forensics platform. It addresses the severe global and regional crisis
of illegal operational bilge dumping and accidental tanker spills across the 
2.37 million km² Indian Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) and global maritime fairways.

Under IMO MARPOL 73/78 Annex I and UNCLOS Article 217, coastal state authorities 
require rigorous, scientifically validated evidence to board, inspect, and penalize 
polluting vessels. SlickMesh AI automates the entire pipeline:
  [Satellite Ingestion] -> [AI Dark-Spot Detection] -> [Lagrangian Hydrodynamic Backtracking] -> 
  [Multi-Signal AIS Evidence Fusion] -> [Actionable MARPOL Legal Investigation Dossiers]

2. FOUR-PHASE SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PHASE 1: SPACEBORNE SATELLITE DETECTION & MULTI-SENSOR QC
- Sensors Integrated:
  1. ESA Sentinel-1 C-SAR (5.405 GHz, VV/VH polarizations): High-resolution radar
     capturing capillary wave damping by oil films (dark surface patches).
  2. ISRO EOS-04 / RISAT-1A (5.35 GHz C-band Quad-Pol SAR): Sovereign Indian radar
     pass providing polarimetric entropy and anisotropy cross-corroboration.
  3. ISRO EOS-06 / Oceansat-3 (OCM-3 Optical + SSTM Thermal IR): Screens out biogenic
     look-alikes (algal blooms via chlorophyll index) and verifies cold-skin thermal delta.
  4. ESA Sentinel-2 MSI (10m VNIR/SWIR): Sunglint optical sheen validation.
  5. Multi-Mission Constellation Fusion (Default): Combines all 4 spaceborne sensors
     simultaneously into a single synthesized composite.
- Deep Learning Architecture: PyTorch Deep U-Net trained on SAR satellite scenes.
- Quality Control & Bonn Agreement:
  * Rejects low-wind (<2.0 m/s) and high-wind (>12.0 m/s) false damping artifacts.
  * Estimates minimum and maximum oil volume (m³ and barrels) using Bonn Agreement Code 3-4.
  * Measures elongation ratio (length/width ~ 3.82) and perimeter morphology.
- Output Contract: Standardized Contract A JSON containing exact polygon vertices,
  centroid lat/lon, detection confidence, and look-alike QC telemetry.

PHASE 2: HYDRODYNAMIC REVERSE-DRIFT BACKTRACKING
- Formulation: Lagrangian numerical reverse simulation over a 1 to 72 hour horizon.
  Net Drift Velocity Vector = Surface Ocean Current (U, V) + 3% Surface Windage Vector.
- Metocean Data Feeds:
  * INCOIS Ocean State Forecasts (OSF) Regional High-Resolution Hydrodynamic Model.
  * ECMWF / Copernicus Marine / Open-Meteo real-time live APIs.
- Origin Calculation: Computes estimated spill origin coordinate at release time (T-horizon),
  together with a dynamic uncertainty radius (km) based on wind speed and elapsed time.

PHASE 3: MULTI-EVIDENCE PROBABILISTIC VESSEL ATTRIBUTION ENGINE
- 4-Stage Fleet Filtering Funnel:
  * Stage 1: Basin Fleet Ingestion (400+ vessels in regional maritime corridor).
  * Stage 2: Spatial Corridor Intersection (filters vessels passing within origin radius).
  * Stage 3: Temporal Window Alignment (matches vessel passage to estimated release time).
  * Stage 4: 7-Signal Kinematic & Behavioral Attribution Scoring.
- 7-Signal Attribution Scoring Matrix (Weighted 0 to 100%):
  1. Metocean Corridor Intersection (Environmental consistency)
  2. Closest Point of Approach (CPA) Distance from Origin
  3. Temporal Window Alignment (Time delta vs release horizon)
  4. AIS Transponder Continuity (Detects deliberate AIS blackout gaps / dark ships)
  5. Heading vs. Slick Elongation Alignment
  6. Speed Profile Consistency (Identifies slow operational discharge speeds 8-14 kts)
  7. Vessel Risk Classification (Crude Tanker > Product/Chemical > Cargo > Fishing)
- Dark Vessel Dead-Reckoning: Performs kinematic interpolation between AIS gap waypoints
  to reconstruct actual passage through the slick origin even if transponder was silenced.

PHASE 4: LIVE COMMAND DASHBOARD & LAW ENFORCEMENT OUTPUTS
- Tactical Live Map (Leaflet.js + CartoDB Dark Matter / Satellite Hybrid):
  * Displays slick polygons, origin uncertainty buffers, and vessel track polylines.
  * Ingests 200 NM Indian EEZ boundary, IMO Traffic Separation Schemes (TSS lanes),
    and ONGC/offshore oil infrastructure (Mumbai High, KG Basin, Panna-Mukta).
- Dual Operation Modes:
  1. Live Satellite Pass Mode: Live coordinate scanning and 361M km² global ocean sweep
     verifying 10 global ocean swaths as clean (0 false alarms) with live AIS traffic.
  2. Forensic Demo Mode: 5 deterministic benchmark case scenarios showcasing forensic
     ranking, reverse drift, and candidate attribution dossiers.
- Forensic Time-Slider: Interactive playback of simulated plume dispersion from T-24h to T=0.
- Outputs & Exports:
  * 1-Click RFC 7946 GeoJSON Export: Full layer bundle for QGIS, ArcGIS, and Coast Guard GIS.
  * 17-Point MARPOL Investigation Brief: Official downloadable markdown audit report
    documenting supporting evidence, counter-evidence, and confidence decomposition.
